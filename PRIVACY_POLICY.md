# Privacy Policy — CaisterPlayz

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 31, 2026  
**Application:** CaisterPlayz (iOS)

CaisterPlayz ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CaisterPlayz mobile application for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information that we receive through our services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Registration:** When you register for an account, we collect your email address, chosen username, display name, and password.
- **Profile Information:** You may choose to provide additional optional information, such as your bio, avatar image, banner image, and user-provided Fortnite in-game name (IGN).
- **User-Generated Content (UGC):** We collect the content you create and post, including gameplay screenshots, video clips, captions, comments, and direct messages.
- **Reports & Support Communications:** When you submit a moderation report or contact customer support, we collect the details of your inquiry.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
- **Session & Device Information:** We collect authentication tokens and basic device information to maintain your session and ensure app security.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the CaisterPlayz app.
- Authenticate users and enforce authorization via Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).
- Enable social interactions (following, liking, commenting, messaging).
- Moderate user-generated content and investigate reports to protect community safety.
- Respond to inquiries and technical support requests.

---

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

- **Public Content:** Your username, display name, avatar, bio, gaming badges, posts, and comments are visible to other users of the platform (unless blocked).
- **Direct Messages:** Private direct messages are only accessible to the participants of that conversation.
- **No Sale of Personal Data:** We do **NOT** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties or advertising brokers.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

All data is stored securely in **Supabase PostgreSQL** infrastructure with database-level Row Level Security (RLS) and encrypted HTTPS/WSS communication. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard auth algorithms; we never store plain-text passwords.

---

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Retention (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In compliance with App Store Guidelines, users have the right to permanently delete their account at any time:
1. Open the **CaisterPlayz** app.
2. Go to **Profile** > **Settings & Safety**.
3. Select **Permanently Delete Account**.
4. Type `DELETE` to confirm.

Upon deletion, your authentication account, profile records, posts, clips, comments, direct messages, and followers will be immediately and permanently removed from our active database.

---

## 6. Age Requirement (13+ Platform)

CaisterPlayz is designed exclusively for users aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for account termination.
